## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-23

[中文版本]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})

### This site (kl-tools-doc)

- This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no analytics
  scripts, ads, or third-party tracking;
- We do not collect or store any personal information about visitors;
- Access data (such as GitHub's server logs) is handled by GitHub in
  accordance with its own privacy policy.

### KL Profile (macOS App)

- Coffee details, roasting parameters, and flavor goals are processed only
  on your device and are not uploaded to the developer;
- Generated `.kpro` files are saved only to locations you choose;
- The app does not require an account and contains no advertising,
  analytics, third-party tracking, or usage-statistics collection;
- The app stores a small amount of data on your device, including a cached
  Flavor Adjustment entitlement and a security-scoped bookmark for the
  Kaffelogic folder you authorize;
- When you view the product, purchase, or restore Flavor Adjustment, the app
  communicates with the App Store through Apple StoreKit. Apple processes
  payments and transaction records; the developer does not receive or store
  payment-card details;
- Help, privacy-policy, GitHub, and other external links open in your default
  browser and are then governed by the destination site's privacy policy.

### kpro-tools

- The Python tool runs locally, does not upload input data, and does not
  collect usage statistics;
- Generated `.kpro` files are saved only to locations you choose.

### Data retention and deletion

- Clearing the app's data removes local preferences and folder authorization;
  deleting the app itself may not automatically remove this local data;
- Exported `.kpro` files are not part of the app's stored data and must be
  deleted manually from their saved locations.

For information about Apple's handling of App Store data, see the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
