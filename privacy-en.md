## Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

[中文版本]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})

### This site (kl-tools-doc)

- This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no analytics
  scripts, ads, or third-party tracking;
- We do not collect or store any personal information about visitors;
- Access data (such as GitHub's server logs) is handled by GitHub in
  accordance with its own privacy policy.

### KL Profile (macOS, iOS, and iPadOS App)

#### Data processing and collection

- Coffee details, roasting parameters, flavor goals, and curve calculations
  are processed only on your device and are not uploaded to the developer;
- The app does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics,
  or third-party tracking. It does not collect usage statistics, device
  identifiers, or location data;
- The app does not use developer-operated servers and does not transmit your
  imported or generated `.kpro` files to the developer.

#### File access and sharing

- On macOS, generated `.kpro` files are saved only to locations you choose.
  When you authorize a Kaffelogic Profile folder, the app stores a
  security-scoped bookmark on your device so it can continue to access that
  folder;
- On iPhone and iPad, the app uses the system file picker to open `.kpro`
  files you explicitly select and the system share sheet to export generated
  files;
- If you choose iCloud Drive or another third-party file provider when
  opening, saving, or sharing a file, that service may process the file under
  its own privacy policy. KL Profile does not independently upload the file
  to the developer.

#### Local settings and In-App Purchases

- The app stores a small amount of preference data on your device, including
  a local cache of the Flavor Adjustment unlock state. These settings are
  used only to provide app functionality;
- When you view the product, purchase, or restore Flavor Adjustment, the app
  communicates with the App Store through Apple StoreKit;
- Apple processes payments, purchase history, and transaction verification.
  The developer does not receive or store your payment-card details;
- Purchase entitlements may be synchronized by the App Store across your Mac,
  iPhone, and iPad when they use the same Apple Account.

#### External links

- Help, privacy-policy, GitHub, and other external links open in the system
  browser and are then governed by the destination site's privacy policy.

### kpro-tools

- The Python tool runs locally, does not upload input data, and does not
  collect usage statistics;
- Generated `.kpro` files are saved only to locations you choose.

### Data retention, control, and deletion

- Local preferences and folder authorizations remain on your device until you
  clear the app's data, revoke the relevant permission, or the operating
  system removes them;
- Apple manages the Flavor Adjustment purchase record. You can use Restore
  Purchases in the app to verify the entitlement again;
- Exported or saved `.kpro` files are not part of the app's internal data and
  must be deleted manually from their saved locations;
- KL Profile does not store personal data on developer-operated servers, so
  there is no cloud account or user profile that you need to ask the
  developer to delete.

For information about Apple's handling of App Store data, see the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
